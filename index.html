QuickTask Account Deletion

This page allows users of the Strength in Numbers app to request account and data deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to strengthinnumbers.app@gmail.com/a>
  2. Include the email address linked to your Strength in Numbers account
  3. We will confirm and process deletion within 7 days

If you prefer, you can also delete your account directly inside the app:
Profile → Settings → Delete Account
